## Daily Health Checklist (Google Tasks, rebuilt around 3 AM)

Every day:
- Weigh in + resting HR + log in Health OS (the task note has the app link)
- Breakfast · Lunch · 2 protein snacks · Dinner
- Water ~125 oz (3.7 L)
- 5-minute mobility
- Wind-down: last check that today is logged

Plus: **Workout** (Thu, Sat) · **Pack UPS snack + extra water bottle** (Mon–Fri) · **Prep tomorrow's lunch** (Sat–Tue).

The app's **Daily quests** card ticks breakfast, lunch, snacks, dinner, protein and water for you as you log food. Unfinished Google Tasks are cleared at 3 AM, so every day starts clean.

## Automatic phone syncs (Shortcuts app)

- When you plug in your iPhone: steps + active calories sent to Health OS.
- Every day at 4:45 PM: same.

## Sleep plan

Wake 5:00. Current lights-out target **10:45**. Every 2 weeks, if it's working, move wind-down and lights-out 15 minutes earlier. After UPS ends, aim for **10:00 → 7 hours**. To change a reminder time, edit the event in Google Calendar and choose "All events".

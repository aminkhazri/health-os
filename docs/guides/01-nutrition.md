# Nutrition Guide — Lean Bulk, Real Food

**Phase 1 targets (provisional):** ~2,600 kcal · protein 145–155 g · carbs ~325 g · fat ~85 g · fiber 30–35 g. **Aim:** gain 0.25–0.5 lb per week, measured by *weekly average*. The tracker rechecks every 2 weeks.

You don't have to count. Type what you ate into the Health OS app and it adds up calories, protein, water and caffeine for you. Hit the four anchors below, with protein at each, and you'll land close to target.

## Logging food in Health OS

- Type it the way you'd say it: "2 eggs, toast and coffee", "half a smoothie", "24 oz water".
- The meal is picked from the time of day (tap the meal label to change it). Drinks go under Drinks.
- If the app doesn't know a food, it asks for rough calories once and remembers it.
- Logging breakfast, lunch, a snack or dinner ticks that daily quest automatically. Reaching ~95% of the protein or water target ticks those too.
- Made a mistake? Tap × twice on the line to remove it.

## Hand-portion plate (no scales)

- **Protein:** 1.5–2 palms (chicken, salmon, eggs, lentils + yogurt)
- **Carbs:** 2 cupped hands (rice, potatoes, oats, tortillas, fruit)
- **Veg:** 1–2 fists
- **Fats:** 1–2 thumbs (cheese, nuts, olive oil, peanut butter)

On workout days and UPS days, add one more cupped hand of carbs.

**Home cooking:** no special meals needed. Take a slightly bigger protein and carb portion of whatever's cooked, and add a glass of whole milk or a yogurt if the dish is light on protein.

## Snack list (portable, calorie-dense)

- Greek yogurt cup + banana (~20 g protein)
- Handful of mixed nuts + string cheese (~12 g)
- Tortilla + 2 tbsp peanut butter + banana, rolled (~12 g, ~450 kcal)
- 2 hard-boiled eggs + fruit (~12 g)
- Glass of whole milk + handful of nuts (~14 g)
- Leftover chicken and rice (~25 g)

## The smoothie (~800 kcal, ~40 g protein, 2 minutes)

1½ cups whole milk · 1 banana (frozen is thicker) · 2 tbsp peanut butter · ½ cup oats · ¾ cup Greek yogurt. Blend 30–45 seconds. Use it when you missed a meal, before UPS, or on hectic mornings. **Half portion = ~400 kcal snack.**

## Emergency food to keep stocked

Greek yogurt cups · whole milk · bananas · peanut butter · mixed nuts · string cheese · whole-wheat tortillas · hard-boiled eggs (boil 6 on the weekend) · oats · tuna or salmon pouches.

**Couldn't eat lunch?** Smoothie now, plus tortilla + peanut butter + banana before UPS.

## Hydration: 125 oz (3.7 L) a day

- Based on the National Academies' reference for adult men (3.7 L of total water a day, for sedentary people), set as the drinks-only target because your days include physical UPS work.
- With a 24 oz bottle that's about **5 refills**: two during the remote shift, one at dinner, two during UPS.
- Coffee, tea and Celsius count toward it.
- Long peak-season shift or a workout? Going past 125 is fine.
- Quick check: pale-yellow urine.

## Caffeine: 400 mg a day maximum

Commonly cited upper limit for healthy adults. Reference amounts in the app: Celsius 250 mg · energy drink 160 mg · coffee 95 mg · espresso 63 mg · tea 47 mg · soda 34 mg. The gauge turns red past 400. Try to keep caffeine before about 2 PM so it doesn't cut into sleep.

## Fiber (30–35 g) without counting

Oats at breakfast + spinach in the wraps + 2 fruits + veg at dinner + lentils a few times a week ≈ 30 g.

# Personal Health OS — Start Here

**Goal:** stop spending mental energy remembering to eat, drink, train, track and recover. Reminders nudge you, one app takes the logging, and the tracker does the maths.

## What's in the system

| Piece | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Health OS app** | Home-screen icon on your iPhone (private link, only you can open it) | The one place to log: weight, sleep, resting heart rate, energy, food and drinks, workouts. Shows gauges, a daily-quest checklist, a streak and progress charts |
| Health & Fitness calendar | Google Calendar app | Timed reminders around your shifts, each with an "Open Health OS" link. Dinner is silent |
| Daily Health Checklist | Google Tasks | Today's to-do list, rebuilt every morning around 3 AM. No alerts; it's a list you open |
| iPhone steps sync | Shortcuts app on your iPhone | Sends today's steps and active calories to the app when you plug in at night and at 4:45 PM |
| Progress Tracker | Google Sheet in this folder | Where all the data lives. Weekly averages, trends and the calorie check calculate themselves |
| Weekly review email | Gmail | Every Sunday around 6 PM: last week's numbers and the 2-week calorie check |
| Guides | This folder | Nutrition, Workout Plan, Reminder Schedule, Automation, UPS Transition |

## Your day (about 2 minutes of logging)

1. **On waking, before coffee:** weigh in, then sit still for a minute and read your heart rate on the watch. Open Health OS and fill in the Morning card: weight, sleep, resting HR, energy. Tap Save.
2. **Every time you eat or drink:** type it in the Food & drinks box, e.g. "2 eggs and coffee". The app works out the calories, files it under the right meal by time of day, and ticks the matching daily quest. Use the quick buttons for water (+8 oz, +24 oz), coffee and Celsius.
3. **Answer reminders when you can.** If you're on a call, do it at the next break. A missed reminder isn't a failure.
4. **Workout days (Thu, Sat):** use the Workout tab. Last session's weights are filled in so you know what to beat.
5. **Wind-down (10:15 PM):** 30-second check that nothing is missing from today.

Skipped a day? Leave it blank. Averages only use the days you logged.

## How to record your weight and heart rate

- Same conditions each day: morning, after the bathroom, before food and water, same scale.
- Daily weight jumps ±2 lb from water and salt. **Only the weekly average matters.**
- Resting heart rate: sitting, relaxed, before coffee. A slow drift down usually means fitness is improving. A jump of 5+ bpm above your normal often means poor sleep, stress or getting sick: a good day to take it easy.

## Weekly review (Sunday 6:30 PM, 10 minutes)

1. Read the weekly email, or open the app's Progress tab.
2. Look at: **weekly weight change**, **workouts (target 2)**, **average sleep**, **average resting HR**, **average steps**.
3. Every 2nd week the **calorie check** says *Add ~150 kcal / Hold / Cut ~150 kcal*. Act on it (below).
4. Pick ONE small focus for next week, not five.

## How to change the calorie target

The check compares your 2-week average gain with the target of 0.25–0.5 lb per week.

- **Add ~150 kcal:** one extra snack daily, e.g. banana + 1 tbsp peanut butter.
- **Cut ~150 kcal:** drop one snack, or halve the smoothie.
- Then in the tracker's **Start Here** tab, update **Daily calories** and add a row to **Calorie history**. The app's gauge follows automatically.
- Never change it more than once per 2 weeks.

## Leaving UPS

Follow **05 UPS Transition Procedure**. A calendar event on **Jan 15, 2027** (provisional) reminds you. Drag it to your real last day once you know it.

## Keeping notifications quiet

- Only the Health & Fitness calendar sends reminders. To mute it during a long call: Google Calendar app → Settings → Health & Fitness → notifications off.
- To silence one reminder for good: open the event → edit → remove its notification → "All events".

## Getting the app back

If you remove the home-screen icon, open the link in any "Health & Fitness" calendar reminder ("Open Health OS") in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen. If the app looks out of date, close it fully and reopen it.

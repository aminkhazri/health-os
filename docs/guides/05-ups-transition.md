# UPS Transition Procedure (Phase 1 → Phase 2)

**Why:** UPS shifts are 3–4+ hours of fast physical work, 5 days a week — a few hundred extra calories a day. When it stops, 2,600 kcal will likely become too big a surplus and the gain will drift toward fat. **Don't carry 2,600 over automatically.**

Triggered by the **UPS transition check** calendar event. Takes ~15 minutes, in your weekly review slot.

## Step 1: Record the change (tracker → Start Here tab)

- Set **Current phase** to "Phase 2: Remote only". This stops the UPS tasks in the daily checklist and updates the label in the app.
- Set **Expected UPS exit** to your real last day.

## Step 2: Calories (provisional reset)

- Lower **Daily calories** to **~2,300** (−300) and add a Calorie history row: "Left UPS — provisional". The app's calorie gauge follows automatically.
- Easiest cut: drop the pre-UPS snack you no longer need.
- Keep protein at **145–155 g**.
- Let the 2-week calorie check fine-tune from there (Add / Hold / Cut).

## Step 3: Check the weight trend

Progress tab or Weekly Summary: was the last month on track (0.25–0.5 lb/week)? If it was already slow, cut less (−150 instead of −300).

## Step 4: Water

UPS days were a big reason for the 125 oz target. Remote-only, about **100 oz** is enough on most days. Drink more on workout days.

## Step 5: Calendar clean-up

- Delete the **Pre-UPS: snack + water + log dinner** series: open one → Delete → "All events". (Move the "log dinner" habit to the wind-down check.)
- Move **Wind-down** to **9:30 PM** and aim for lights-out **10:00** (7 hours with a 5:00 wake).

## Step 6: Training

- If the Week-4 check passed, add a **3rd workout**: **Mon or Tue at 5:45 PM**, keeping Thu + Sat.
- Re-run the Week-4 criteria after 3 weeks at the new frequency.

## Step 7: Replace the lost activity

- Your steps already sync to the app. Watch the 7-day average steps on the Progress tab; it will drop when UPS ends.
- Turn one break reminder into a **10-minute walk** on workdays, and aim to keep the 8,000-step goal.

## Step 8: Re-check after 4 weeks

In the weekly review 4 weeks later: weight trend, energy, sleep, resting HR. Adjust calories through the normal 2-week check.

**Easiest option:** tell Claude "I left UPS on [date], run the transition". It can make the calendar and tracker changes for you.

Morning ritual · about 10 minutes, same order every day
  1. Bathroom, then weigh in
  2. Sit still 1 minute: slow breaths (in 4, out 6) while the watch reads your heart rate
  3. Log weight, sleep, heart rate and energy above
  4. Big glass of water
  5. Wake-up stretch: open the Stretch tab and press Start Wake-up (5 min)
  6. Breakfast, and log it
